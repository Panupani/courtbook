1. Getting Started

Create an account, log in, and learn your way around the app

1.1 Creating an Account

Open your browser and navigate to the CourtBook website.
Click Sign Up in the top navigation bar.
Enter your full name, email address, and a password (minimum 8 characters).
Optionally add your phone number — this helps staff contact you if needed.
Click Create Account. You will be logged in automatically.
💡
Tip Your email address is used to log in and receive booking notifications. Make sure it is correct.

1.2 Logging In

Click Log In in the navigation bar.
Enter your email and password.
Click Sign In. You will be redirected to your dashboard.
⚠️
Forgot your password? Click "Forgot password?" on the login page and follow the email link to reset it.

1.3 Navigating the App

🏠

Home / Venues

Browse all available venues and their courts from the main page.

📅

My Bookings

View your upcoming and past bookings from the Dashboard.

👤

Profile

Update your name and phone number from your account menu.

🔓

Log Out

Click your avatar in the top-right corner and select Sign Out.

2. Booking a Court

Customer guide — from browsing to confirmed booking

2.1 Browsing Venues & Courts

From the home page, click Browse Venues or select a featured venue card.
Click on a venue to view its courts, address, and facilities.
Click Book Now next to the court you want.

2.2 Selecting Date & Time Slots

The booking grid shows all courts at a venue for a chosen date. You can select multiple slots and even book multiple courts in one transaction.

Use the date strip at the top to scroll through the next 14 days and tap the date you want.
Each court shows its available time slots. Tap a slot to add it to your cart. Tap again to remove it.
You can select slots from multiple courts on the same date — they will be grouped into one booking.
When ready, tap Proceed to Payment in the sticky bar at the bottom.
Slot Legend
Slot ColourMeaning
⬜ White borderAvailable — tap to select
🟢 Green filledSelected by you — in your cart
🟠 Orange borderAvailable but peak pricing applies
⬛ Grey / strikethroughAlready booked by someone else — unavailable
ℹ️
Peak pricing — Some courts charge a higher rate during peak hours (e.g. evenings and weekends). The price is displayed on every slot tile so you always know the cost before confirming.

2.3 Checkout & Payment (PromptPay)

Review the Order Summary showing each slot and the total amount due.
A PromptPay QR code is displayed. Open your banking app and scan it, or enter the PromptPay number manually.
Transfer exactly the amount shown (tolerance ±฿2). Do not round up or down.
Take a screenshot of the transfer success screen inside your banking app.
⏱️
5-Minute Timer — The checkout page shows a countdown. You must upload your payment slip within 5 minutes of the transfer for automatic AI verification. The timer turns amber at 2 minutes and red at 1 minute remaining.
🧾

2. Booking a Court (continued)

Uploading your slip and receiving confirmation

2.4 Uploading Your Payment Slip

Tap the 📎 Upload Slip area on the checkout page.
Select the screenshot from your photo gallery or camera. Accepted formats: JPG, PNG, WebP.
Preview the image — make sure the amount, date, and transfer status (โอนสำเร็จ) are clearly visible.
Tap ✓ Verify & Confirm Booking.
Wait a few seconds while the AI checks your slip. The button shows a progress spinner.
💡
What if the timer expired before I could upload? The button changes to 📋 Submit for Manual Review. You can still upload your slip — the booking will be held as pending and confirmed by staff once they review it.

2.5 Booking Confirmation

If the AI successfully verifies your slip, you are taken to the Booking Confirmation page showing:

  • ✅ "Booking Confirmed!" banner
  • Booking reference number (e.g. A1B2C3D4)
  • Venue, court, date, and time
  • Total paid
  • Link to view your full booking group (if multiple slots)

⚠️
Slip rejected? The AI checks that the amount matches, the slip shows today's date, and the transfer is complete (not pending). Common reasons for rejection:
  • Wrong transfer amount
  • Slip shows "pending" status instead of "success / โอนสำเร็จ"
  • Slip is from a previous day
  • Image is blurry or cropped
Upload a clear, complete slip and try again. If it keeps failing, contact venue staff.

3. My Bookings Dashboard

Track and manage all your reservations

3.1 Viewing Upcoming Bookings

Access your dashboard by clicking My Bookings in the navigation bar. The page is divided into two sections:

  • Upcoming — bookings from today onwards (excludes cancelled)
  • Past Bookings — historical records (last 20 entries)

Each row shows the court name, venue, date/time span, total price, and current status. Click any row to open the full booking detail page.

3.2 Booking Status Guide

StatusMeaningNext Action
confirmed Payment verified. Slot is reserved for you. Show up and play! 🏸
pending Slip submitted, awaiting verification (AI or manual). Wait for staff confirmation. Usually instant.
cancelled Booking was cancelled by staff or the system. Contact the venue if this was unexpected.

3.3 Group Bookings

When you book multiple slots (different courts or consecutive times) in one transaction, they form a Group Booking. On the dashboard they appear as a single row with a X slots badge.

Click the row to open the Group Booking page, which shows all individual slots, each court's subtotal, and the grand total. From there you can also click + Add More Slots to append additional courts to the same group.

ℹ️
One payment for the whole group — When you pay for a group booking, a single slip covers all slots. The AI checks the total amount against the combined price of all selected slots.

4. Admin Panel — Overview

For venue owners and system administrators

4.1 Accessing the Admin Panel

Log in with your admin account credentials.
Navigate to /admin (e.g. https://yoursite.com/admin) or click the Admin link in the navigation menu.
If access is denied, your account does not have an admin role. Contact a system administrator.
🔒
Security notice — The admin panel is protected by role-based access. Admin roles are assigned directly in the database by a system administrator and cannot be self-assigned.

4.2 Admin Roles

RoleAccess LevelScope
System Admin Full access to everything All venues, all bookings, all payments
Venue Admin Venue-scoped access Only bookings & payments for assigned venues

Venue admins see exactly the same admin interface but all data is automatically filtered to their assigned venue(s). They cannot see bookings from other venues.

4.3 Dashboard Overview

The admin dashboard (/admin) shows at-a-glance statistics:

📅

Total number of bookings for the current day across all (assigned) courts.

💰

Revenue

Total confirmed revenue for the current month.

⏳

Number of PromptPay slips awaiting review. Click to jump to the queue.

The left sidebar provides quick navigation to all admin sections: Bookings, Pending Payments, Venues, Courts, and Schedule.

5. Managing Venues & Courts

Set up your facilities, pricing, and operating hours

5.1 Adding a Venue

Go to Admin → Venues and click + New Venue.
Fill in the Venue Name, Address, and optional Description.
Enter your PromptPay ID (phone number or 13-digit national ID). This generates QR codes for customer payments.
Set the Platform Fee Rate (default 5%). This is the percentage deducted from each booking as a service fee.
Toggle Active to make the venue visible to customers.
Click Save Venue.

5.2 Adding Courts

Go to Admin → Courts and click + New Court.
Select the Venue this court belongs to.
Enter a Court Name (e.g. "Court A", "Main Hall").
Set the Hourly Rate (฿) — this is the base off-peak rate.
Toggle Active and click Save Court.

5.3 Setting Operating Hours

Go to Admin → Schedule. Select a court and configure its hours per day of the week.

FieldDescriptionExample
Open TimeFirst slot starts at this time08:00
Close TimeLast slot ends at this time22:00
Slot DurationLength of each booking slot (minutes)60

Days without an entry in the schedule are treated as closed — no slots are generated and the court appears as unavailable.

5.4 Peak Pricing

Each day's schedule can have an optional peak window. During peak hours a higher rate applies automatically.

FieldDescription
Peak Start / EndTime range when peak pricing applies (e.g. 17:00 – 21:00)
Peak Hourly Rate (฿)Higher price per hour during the peak window
💡
Slots that overlap the peak boundary are split automatically — the customer pays the correct prorated amount for each portion.

View, filter, walk-in book, and cancel reservations

6.1 Viewing All Bookings

Go to Admin → Bookings. The table shows all bookings with these columns:

Date & TimeBooking date and slot time range
TotalAmount charged for this slot

6.2 Filtering & Searching

Use the filter bar above the table to narrow down results:

  • Status — filter by confirmed, pending, cancelled, or all
  • Date — show bookings for a specific date only

The table loads the latest 100 bookings by default, ordered newest first.

6.3 Walk-in Bookings

Walk-in bookings are for customers who pay cash on-site. They are created directly by staff and confirmed immediately — no payment slip required.

Click 🚶 Walk-in Booking at the top of the Bookings page.
Select the venue, court, and date.
Available time slots are loaded automatically. Select one or more.
Optionally enter customer name and phone for your records.
Click Confirm Walk-in. The booking is immediately set to confirmed with payment method "Cash".

6.4 Cancelling a Booking

⚠️
Cancellations are permanent. Once a booking is cancelled it cannot be restored. The slot becomes available for other customers immediately.

To cancel: click the Cancel link in the Action column and confirm the dialog prompt. Cancelled bookings remain visible in the table with a cancelled badge for record-keeping.

7. Payment Management

Review PromptPay slips, approve payments, and use AI verification

7.1 Pending Payments Queue

Go to Admin → Pending Payments. This page shows all bookings that:

  • Used PromptPay as the payment method
  • Have a pending payment status

Each card shows the payment slip image (if uploaded), customer details, court, date/time, and amount. Cards are ordered newest first.

ℹ️
Auto-cleared — Most slips are verified automatically by AI within seconds of submission. Only slips that failed AI verification (or were submitted after the timer expired) appear in this queue.

7.2 Approving a Slip Manually

Click the slip image to open it full size. Verify that the amount, date, and transfer status are correct.
Click ✓ Approve to confirm the payment. The booking status changes to confirmed and the customer's slot is secured.
Alternatively, click ✕ Reject if the slip is invalid (wrong amount, fake, or from a different transaction). The booking stays as pending for re-submission or manual follow-up.

7.3 AI Re-verification

If a slip looks valid but was rejected automatically, use the 🤖 Re-verify AI button to run a second AI check.

Click 🤖 Re-verify AI on the pending payment card.
Wait a few seconds while the AI analyses the slip image.
The result appears inline:
✓ AI approved — booking is automatically confirmed.
✕ AI rejected — reason is shown; you can still approve manually.
💡
Admin re-verify vs customer verify: The admin AI check does NOT enforce the 5-minute time window — it only checks the date (today) and the correct amount. This allows reviewing slips that were submitted hours earlier.

7.4 Viewing Slips in Bookings Table

The main Bookings table also has a Slip column. Click the 🧾 Slip button on any row to open a popup showing the payment image. This works for both pending and confirmed bookings, making it easy to audit historical payments without leaving the bookings page.

💡
Click ↗ Open full size in the slip popup to view the original high-resolution image in a new tab.

8. Troubleshooting

Common issues and how to resolve them

8.1 Customer Issues

ProblemLikely CauseFix
Cannot log in Wrong password or email Use "Forgot Password?" link on the login page
Slot I want is greyed out Already booked by another customer Choose a different time slot or date
QR code doesn't scan PromptPay ID not configured for this venue Enter the PromptPay number manually in your banking app
Slip rejected — wrong amount Transferred a different sum Transfer the exact amount shown. Contact staff if already transferred.
Slip rejected — pending status Screenshot taken before transfer completed Wait for the transfer to complete (โอนสำเร็จ) then screenshot again
Timer expired before upload Took longer than 5 minutes Upload anyway — click "Submit for Manual Review". Staff will confirm.
Booking stuck at "pending" AI could not process slip / submitted after timer Wait for staff to review, or contact the venue directly

8.2 Admin Issues

ProblemLikely CauseFix
Access denied to /admin Account role is not admin or venue_admin Ask a system admin to set the correct role in the database
Walk-in form shows no courts No courts assigned to your venue Add courts in Admin → Courts, then assign them to your venue
No slots appear in walk-in Operating hours not set for that day Configure schedule in Admin → Schedule for the selected court & day
Slip shows "No slip image stored" Customer submitted slip but upload failed Ask customer to re-submit. Check storage bucket settings.
Pending payments queue is empty but bookings are pending Pending bookings used cash (not PromptPay) Walk-in cash bookings don't appear in the payments queue — they're already confirmed

8.3 Payment Verification Issues

ProblemCauseFix
🤖 Re-verify AI returns error Gemini API key not configured or quota exceeded Visit /api/admin/test-gemini to diagnose. Approve manually.
AI always says "slip older than 5 minutes" Using old customer verify endpoint, not admin endpoint Use the 🤖 Re-verify AI button — admin endpoint has no time window
Correct slip rejected by AI Image quality, unusual bank app format, or Buddhist Era date Click ✓ Approve manually. The AI is advisory, not mandatory.
